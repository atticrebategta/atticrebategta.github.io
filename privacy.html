Privacy

Attic Rebate GTA. Last updated 23 September 2026.

This page explains what we do with the information you give us. It is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Only what you hand us yourself: your name, phone number, email address, postal code, and your answers about your home, such as whether you own it, how it is heated, roughly when it was built, and whether you have had an attic insulation rebate before.

If you reach us through a form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta passes us the details you confirmed in that form. Meta's own handling of your data is covered by their privacy policy, not this one.

What we do with it

  • Work out whether your home qualifies for the Ontario Home Renovation Savings Program.
  • Reply to you, normally by text message.
  • Pass your details to the insulation contractor who would do the work, so they can arrange a visit and quote. We tell you who they are before we do this.

That is all. We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not pass it to anyone other than the contractor handling your job.

How long we keep it

Until the job is finished or it is clear nothing is going ahead, and then no longer than a year. Ask us to delete it sooner and we will.

Who we are, plainly

We arrange attic insulation work. We do not install it ourselves, and we are not Enbridge, Save on Energy, the IESO, or the Government of Ontario, and we are not acting on their behalf. The installation is carried out by a contractor participating in the programme. The programme's own terms, including the rebate amount, are set by its administrators and can change.

Contact

Text 249-499-4585, or message the Attic Rebate GTA page on Facebook. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, ask us on either and we will action it.
